# UltraX Video — End-to-End AI Video Creation Skill

> **Let video creation return to content and creativity — leave the tools and complexity to the Agent.**

`uxvideo` is an end-to-end video-creation skill that any AI agent can load. It drives a fully
private generation stack to take a project from idea to finished cut — content planning,
storyboard & shot design, video generation, voiceover, and background music.

> 🏆 This project grew out of our **finalist entry in the NVIDIA DGX Spark Hackathon**.

▶ Watch the product intro:

[![UltraX Video intro](assets/intro/pipeline_models.png)](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1UZgy6qEeT/)

## Highlights

- **End-to-end pipeline** — content planning → storyboard & shot creation → video generation → voiceover → background music, in one flow.
- **Many video genres** — digital human (talking head), storytelling, MV, vlog, interview, and more.
- **Fully private / on-premise** — runs on GPUs with under 16 GB VRAM, including 12 GB cards.
- **Flexible output** — multiple aspect ratios (9:16, 16:9, 3:4) and 480p video creation.
- **2K image generation.**
- **Emotion-aware, multi-speaker voiceover.**
